## Roblox policy notes
- Third-party IP: valid takedowns remove content; DMCA counter-notice takes ~14 business days; repeat infringement can lead to suspension. [DMCA guidelines](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/publishing/dmca-guidelines)
- Real political figures and recreations of real conflicts are banned, so the Mayor of Chaos City replaces any real politician.
- Weapons and explosions are fine if they're cartoony. No real aircraft, national insignia or real nations.
- Maturity: cartoon knockouts are "Mild". Fill in the questionnaire, or the game is restricted. [Content maturity](https://create.roblox.com/docs/production/promotion/content-maturity)
- Terms of Use update (Nov 1, 2026): no IP changes described. Minors need a parent or guardian to accept the new terms.

## Rule for this project
- Build every model, voice line and piece of music ourselves. Never copy viral images or clips.
- LOW names can be used freely.
- Every HIGH or MEDIUM character gets a rhyming fake-Italian parody name and a clearly different design.
- Never put a claimed character's name in the game title or thumbnail.
